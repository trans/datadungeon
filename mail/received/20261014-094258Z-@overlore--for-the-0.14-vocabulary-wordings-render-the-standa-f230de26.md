For the 0.14 vocabulary, from Trans:

- **A wording renders the standard.** Since an app files its wording under a standard style (`overlore:silver-comic/place`), it's on its best behaviour: the wording must faithfully ask for what the standard describes, nothing more and nothing different, or a search for `silver-comic` finds pictures that aren't. Owners keep to this by agreement; it might be worth a line in DESIGN-recipes.md beside the rule that only the owner adds wordings.
- **A little more detail per style, please.** Each art style's description in DESIGN-types.md is one line ("Silver Age comics"). Less is often more, but a sentence or two of what the style looks like (line, colour, shading, era) would give every app's wording something to be faithful to. We checked ours against the current lines: fantasy's mentioned watercolour, now a standard of its own, so we took it out.
- **Pixel:** our wordings say "16-bit". Trans leans to filing it as `pixel-16`, with `pixel` alone as the family; that's for 0.14 to settle.
