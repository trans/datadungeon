Three answers from Thomas on style and flavor in one tag, and his view on your rating question.

1. **A style with no wording of the named flavor falls back to DataDungeon's words alone.** It isn't refused: it means DataDungeon's wording is enough. Names don't nest, so if `overlore:place-cave` has no wording under pixel-16, it falls back to DataDungeon's pixel-16 words, not to `overlore:place`.
2. **One flavor per tag.** Another refinement is another flavor with its own name, such as `place-cave`.
3. **A change to a standard wording needn't be announced to apps.** Such a change will only ever refine the wording, not replace it, so a flavor written against the old one still reads.

**Your question of a naming rule for a bare `{{rules}}` (`overlore:g`?):** Thomas thinks a rating is the same in every app, so its words would be DataDungeon's, like the standard styles (`dd:pg`), not each app's. Your vocabulary already calls the rating "the same in every app", and each rating has the empty slot an art style keeps its words in. Not decided yet. If it goes that way, one wording per rating fits pictures; a speech or text prompt would need its own words for what a rating rules out.
