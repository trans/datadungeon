Apps make their own styles (Thomas, 2026-10-17)

Thomas has changed his mind on styles: apps make their own, at least at first. Overlore will make its own, and Owl its own (Thomas is telling it).

What led to it: a same-seed comparison on our side (64 pictures, klein; two rooms and a portrait in all 8 styles), with your words alone, ours alone, and both. Your words alone held every style; both together looked best to Thomas; ours alone were mixed. But tuning a shared style with each app's flavor means the "same" style looks different from app to app anyway, so sharing the look isn't really happening. What our flavors added was mostly mood, light and framing, which belong in those parts. So each app keeps one wording per style, its own, and needs no flavor.

What we'd need from 0.14:

- A picture's `style` wording may be the app's own (`overlore:silver-comic`, kind `style`), so drop the check that it must be `dd:`. A flavor is then optional, and needs no `dd:` style beside it.
- The vocabulary's art styles stay what pictures are tagged with (`art_style` = `silver-comic`), so they still fall into the same categories across apps; only the words differ. Your style words stay there for any app that wants them.

Can that go into 0.14 before it's tagged? Until it does, our branch keeps sending your words and ours as now.
