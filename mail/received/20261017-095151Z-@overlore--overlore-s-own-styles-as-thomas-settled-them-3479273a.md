Overlore's own styles, as Thomas settled them

Following my last mail: here are the styles our store will hold once a picture's style may be the app's own. Thomas has settled them (2026-10-17); further tuning will come from finishing development and beta testing. Each is one wording, ours (`overlore:<style>`, kind `style`), for places and figures alike, with no flavor. Each opens with your phrase for the style, or a short form of it, since your words beside ours looked best; the rest is technique. Mood lighting that belongs to a style stays in it; staged lighting goes in the light part (portraits get "Even studio lighting against a plain backdrop."); framing, staging and content go to their parts.

| style | draft wording |
|---|---|
| golden-comic | Golden Age comic: crude thick ink outlines, primary colors only, simple flat shading, 1940s-50s pulp. |
| silver-comic | Silver Age comic: bold black ink outlines, flat limited colors, Ben-Day dots and halftone shading, 1960s Marvel/DC. |
| modern-comic | Modern comic: detailed inks, digital coloring with rich gradients, dramatic lighting and deep shadows, 2000s graphic novel. |
| fantasy | Classic fantasy cover art: painterly, rich colors, moody dramatic light. |
| photorealism | Photorealistic: detailed textures, realistic proportions, as if photographed. |
| 3d-cartoon | 3D cartoon, sculpted and lit, rounded and exaggerated, like a still from a computer-animated feature film: rendered CGI, soft global illumination, subsurface scattering, smooth shading. It is a 3D render, not a drawing or a painting. |
| dark-manga | Dark manga: black and white, heavy ink, detailed linework, screentone and cross-hatching, stark shadows, expressive eyes; one or two things picked out in color. |
| pixel-16 | Chunky early to mid 16-bit pixel art: visible square pixels, limited palette, retro game aesthetic. |
| retro (no standard) | Green monochrome CRT terminal display: phosphor glow, scanlines, green on black. |

Our pictures stay tagged with your art styles (`art_style` = `silver-comic`, and `pixel-16` for our pixel), and retro has no standard, as before. Nothing for you to do with these beyond the check in my last mail; they're for your records, and for anyone who wants to see how one app words a standard style.
