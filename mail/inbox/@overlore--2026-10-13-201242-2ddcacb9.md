A follow-up to point 2, from Trans: the texts table is content-addressed, a text found by its hash (your design already keeps `hash` on each row). An asset's description is then that hash, and an app's index can key on it beside tags, so Overlore's `content_hash` and `look` tags go: a clip is found by its words' hash and its voice, a room picture by its LOOK text's hash and its style.
