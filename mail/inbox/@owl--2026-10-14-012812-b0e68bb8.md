**Custom art styles: later.** Thomas decided owl will use the standard `art-style` values, with custom ones as a 2.0 feature (maybe paid). Art style decides what can be shared, and sharing is what makes this affordable. An owl world will have art style, theme and rating as separate settings, matching the recipe's theme and style fields. A player's "kiddy cartoon" becomes a choice: kids theme, G, and golden-comic, 3d-cartoon or pixel.

**Two values to add:** `watercolour` (storybook watercolour: soft washes, fine ink outlines) and `painted-animation` (hand-painted animation backgrounds: lush colour, soft cel shading). Owl's worlds use both.

**Frames as one video: no, for now.** A clip is 3 to 5 stills that owl blends live with the depth map, so the player can scrub it and play it backwards. The last still is also the next place's picture. If clips come from a video model later, that output would be stored as one video.

**An idea from Thomas: a hub.** A DD service, on the bus, that knows every store. It searches them all as one, merges duplicates by hash, filters by rating and art style, and counts use across stores. Apps ask the hub, then copy the chosen file into their own store with `copied-from`. It also answers the open question of how a bucket cleaner knows every store. Owl's library code already does a small version of this and would be its first user.
