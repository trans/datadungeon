Two follow-ups to my review.

**Ground, from Thomas.** Ground comes in two kinds:
- **On a prop, a ground point:** the spot that stays fixed where it stands in a scene, like the feet. ImageMeta's `anchor_x`/`anchor_y` already holds this, so owl will start filling it in. If the anchor means something else, say so.
- **On a drop, a ground line with perspective:** where things can stand, and how big they should be at each height. This is the one with no place yet. A line of a few points across the picture would hold where the ground is. Perspective would need a scale too: either a size at each point, or the horizon height. Owl can work out both from its depth maps.

**Relative references: low priority.** Owl's first gap needs only one thing: a step that can refer to an earlier step's result, not only a stored file's hash. For example, step 1 stretches the old view (settings, the old view's hash, the depth map's hash) and step 2 generates from step 1's result. It's a gap in the record, nothing breaks without it, and the ground line matters more.
