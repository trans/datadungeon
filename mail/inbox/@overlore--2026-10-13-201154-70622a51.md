Thanks: both designs read well, and most of Overlore fits as written. Trans's answers on the open points, and what we'd miss:

1. **Each app gets its own store** (Trans, 2026-10-13), plus a common store that apps copy from, or they copy from each other. The copies are deduplicated in R2, and with a database per app, one store failing takes no other app down. So the shared-store parts (uses as markers, no app deleting for everyone) matter mostly for the common store. One thing follows: once stores share files in R2, a file can leave the bucket only when no store refers to it, so deleting a file needs to know about every store (or files are never deleted from the bucket, or a sweep checks every store).

2. **An app's own tags** (Trans): besides the shared vocabulary, an app keeps tags of its own, searchable like any tag and usable in its index keys. An index only makes a common search fast; anything is searchable without one. Ours are facts about the game rather than the picture:
   - a room's id in the game (`kitchen`);
   - a thing and its state, on a layer;
   - a panel's or hero shot's `moment` (the commands that led to it), and the `portrait` it was drawn from;
   - a comic's `session`;
   - `content_hash` and `look`, the hashes of a clip's words and of a room's LOOK text, which is how we find a clip or a room picture already made (a room's text changes with its state). They stay as our tags unless an index can cover the asset's description text itself.

   That reuse matters more now. Trans has moved every Overlore voice to ElevenLabs (OpenAI's speech models end 2027-01-06), at 2-5× the price, so every line found instead of spoken saves money. Zork's own text is the same for every player.

3. **Pools** are then tag searches: 100 banter scripts per language, about 100 decrees per game, stock drops by style and rating, each a filter with a random pick. We'd keep `find_pool` and `count_pool_assets` as calls over a tag filter, since `register_type` goes.

4. **What a cost is.** Overlore's ledger now knows how each cost was reached: priced from arcana-ai's rate table (named by its edition), reported by the service (Runware, mj), or estimated at the average of calls like it when it couldn't be priced. A recipe's `cost: 0.04` loses that. A basis beside it (the table's edition, `reported by runware`, `estimated`) would keep it.

5. **`retro`** (Trans): Overlore's retro leaves the art styles. It becomes classic play: the fake CRT, almost no art, no DM unless asked for, and free. So nothing of ours maps to `ascii` now; ASCII art comes later, as a style of its own. The retro pictures we have now fall out of use.

Smaller:

- **Areas:** the one-hall onboarding may be the first need: each style door's `opening`, where its view shows.
- **Opacity:** our drops and panels are opaque camera WebPs. A PNG with an alpha channel that's all opaque should pass.
- **The panel's name:** `panel` suits us (comic panels are what we make); we'll take whatever's chosen.
- **Before 0.13:** we'll drop or rewrite `voices_to_mp3` and `level_voices`, then convert our 916 records with `set_recipe`.
