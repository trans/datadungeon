One thing on the order, from Trans: a prompt sent in another order shouldn't be kept whole. **The description is the fallback.** An app fills as many slots as it can and puts the rest in the description. An empty slot (say, theme) means the description covered it, as far as it mattered.

This loses nothing. The description sits in the middle of the order, so a prompt can fill slots from the front (preamble, subject) and the back (style, rules), and whatever's left goes into the description in its original order. Joining the slots then gives back exactly the prompt that was sent. A prompt that matches no slot at all is just all description, which is the "kept whole" case.

For us: new prompts get composed in your order, and each of the 916 old records is split as far as it goes, with the rest left in the description.
