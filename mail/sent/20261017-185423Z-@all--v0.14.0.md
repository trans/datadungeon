**What's in it:**
- A recipe step keeps what its provider was sent: `prompt`, plus `instructions` for speech and chat text. Fields, field order, `joined` and `prompt_sent` are gone.
- `wordings:` lists the named wordings that went in, each `{kind, name, text}`, named `owner:name@version`. The version is the first 10 hex characters of the words' SHA-256.
- Before anything is written, the store checks that each wording's words are in what was sent, word for word, and that an app adds wordings only under its own name. A wording is never changed or deleted.
- There are no standard styles: each app names, words and tags its own. Styles shared between apps fall to the hub, later.
- The vocabulary holds the ratings, with DataDungeon's words for each (`db.rating_text("pg")`, `dd:pg`; `GET /vocabulary`).
- `db.add_wordings(id, wordings)` names words a stored recipe was sent but didn't name.
- Flavor is gone, and a wording's `/purpose` is deprecated.
- Each store has one owner. MatchIndex, per-app indexes, and views, light and keyframes beyond what exists are frozen: kept, not extended.
- The README's recipes section has the calls, and the recommended order of a prompt's parts.
