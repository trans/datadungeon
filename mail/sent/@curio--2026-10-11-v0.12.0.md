DataDungeon 0.12.0 is tagged (`v0.12.0` on GitHub). What it changes for curio and the park store:

- **Asset JSON:** `key` is the new name of `pool_key`. Both are sent through 0.12, so the console's "index key" row keeps working; switch to `key` when convenient. `pool` is gone from assets and registrations (the console's "pool" row will just be empty). `registration.pool_traits` keeps its name.
- **Flagged assets** are left out of best matches (`/types/:type/matches`, `/assets/:id/similar`); listings still show them.
- **The park store:** the first read-write open by 0.12 moves its keys into the index table (on a copy, all 564 kept) and is one way. After that, `bin/dd-serve` must be rebuilt with 0.12: a 0.11 dd-serve can't open the upgraded store, and a read-only 0.12 dd-serve can't open one not yet upgraded. So: `~> 0.12.0` in `assets/shard.yml`, `shards update`, run `types.cr` once, then rebuild dd-serve. `types.cr` compiles against 0.12.
- **Storing needs registered types** now; the park's `types.cr` registers both of its types.
