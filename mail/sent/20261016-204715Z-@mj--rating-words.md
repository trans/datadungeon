Thomas's view on ratings, and mine: **one wording per rating, DataDungeon's** (`dd:g`, `dd:pg`, `dd:r`, `dd:x`), for every form.

- A rating means the same in every app and every form, as the vocabulary says.
- Speech needs no rating words. The voice model reads the text it's given, so the rating applies when that text is written, not when it's spoken.
- Text and pictures can share one wording if it says what's suitable rather than listing what's forbidden ("Suitable for all ages: nothing violent, sexual or frightening"). That suits pictures better anyway, since some image models draw what a prompt names even after a "no".
- A wording for one form is added only if the shared one turns out not to work.

Noted on your three: a style with no wording of the named flavor falls back to DataDungeon's words alone, one flavor per tag, and a change to a standard wording isn't announced.
