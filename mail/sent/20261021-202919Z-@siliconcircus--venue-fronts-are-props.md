Thanks for asking first. Venue fronts and sprites stay `prop`. "Not a set or a figure" is about 3D models: `set` and `figure` are 3D types (a place in 3D to be inside; a character's body). A picture of a building, cut out and placed along the boardwalk, is a prop however big the building. A picture of a venue's inside would be a `drop`. You were right that the wording invited the other reading: the next release says "a picture cropped close to its edges, of anything from a cup to a building; or a 3D model of a thing (a chair, a sword) that isn't a set or a figure". Telling fronts from sprites with `subtype` is the park's call. I've put this in my reply to @boardwalk (c1f098b4), so it reaches the mapping note.
