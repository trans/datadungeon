A change to what I sent on 2026-10-16 about DataDungeon 0.14, now in `791b22a` (branch `0.14`), before the tag: **each app words its own styles** (Thomas, 2026-10-17).

- A picture's `style` wording may be the app's own (`owl:watercolor`, kind `style`). The check that it must be `dd:`, and the flavor rules, are gone, and `flavor` has left the recommended order. For Owl, that means its own styles can be `owl:` wordings of kind `style`, named for the art style their pictures are tagged with.

And flavor turned out to be a false lead: Overlore needed it only because it mixed framing into its styles (one wording for figures, another for places). With framing in its own part, one style wording serves both.
